## Macy's discount tiers, decoded

Macy's sale pages use a vocabulary that looks like noise until you know the hierarchy. From steepest to most routine:

- **Last Act** — the deepest clearance tier, on items nearly sold through. Discounts can top 80%; brands like Calvin Klein, Tommy Hilfiger, and Michael Kors commonly sit at 70–80% off here.
- **Today Only** — expires at midnight, steepest discounts of the day, especially jewelry.
- **Clearance** — final markdowns on end-of-season goods. Prices generally won't drop further, so waiting doesn't pay.
- **Limited-Time Specials** — a few days to two weeks, common on men's suits and outerwear.

Macy's also groups sale items by price — $10 and under, $25 and under, $50 and under, $100 and under — which is faster than filtering by discount percentage when you're shopping with a fixed budget.

Macy's also offers Klarna, splitting purchases into four interest-free payments every two weeks.
